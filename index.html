Last Updated: October 28, 2026

Welcome to CrazyCal. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the CrazyCal mobile application.

CrazyCal is designed to help users perform calculations. The app's information practices depend on the features enabled in the version you use. We do not intentionally collect personal information through basic calculations.

Information, if collected by features of the app, may be used to provide app functionality, maintain performance, fix technical problems, and improve the user experience. We do not sell personal information.

3. Data Storage

Calculation results or other data you enter may be stored on your device if the app provides such functionality. Please review the app's behavior before entering sensitive or confidential information.

4. Permissions

CrazyCal should request device permissions only when needed for a feature. You can review and manage app permissions through your device settings.

5. Third-Party Services

If the app uses third-party services, those providers may process information according to their own privacy policies. Details of any such services should be added here when applicable.

6. Children's Privacy

CrazyCal does not knowingly collect personal information from children through its basic calculation functionality. If you believe personal information has been collected improperly, please contact us.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by the app. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's features or information practices change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised update date.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the app developer using the contact information provided on the CrazyCal app's Google Play Store listing.
